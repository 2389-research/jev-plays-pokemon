# Kanto Overworld Map — Pallet Town to Pewter City Corridor

This document is the navigation map for the Pallet Town to Pewter City route in Pokémon Red. Each area lists the adjacent areas you can travel to and the compass direction of travel. Directions are ground truth (map-edge connections from the game data; gate/door exits from the exit tile's position). Coordinates are the exit tile on the current map.

## Pallet Town
- Pallet Town connects **north** to **Route 1** (exit tile at x=1, y=0).
- Pallet Town connects **south** to **Map 32** (exit tile at x=2, y=17).

## Viridian City

- Viridian City connects **north** to **Map 45** (exit tile at x=32, y=7).
- Viridian City connects **south** to **Route 1** (exit tile at x=20, y=35).
